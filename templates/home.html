Welcome to the Vital Tracker Web Server!

Our senior design project focuses on creating a low-cost IoT-enabled patient monitoring system that continuously tracks key vital signs while minimizing disturbances from traditional practices and devices. The system securely transmits real-time data to healthcare providers, allowing nurses and doctors to remotely monitor overnight patients, respond faster to changes, and improve overall patient comfort and care effieciency.
